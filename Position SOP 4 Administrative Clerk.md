# Administrative Clerk SOP

## 1. Position

**1.1** The position governed by this SOP is Administrative Clerk.

**1.2** The Administrative Clerk is an operating position within XOS Administrative Services.

**1.3** This SOP defines the current responsibilities, authority, limitations, and operating requirements of the Administrative Clerk position.

**1.4** This SOP does not define the broader development of Administrative Services and does not establish a future title or promotion path.

## 2. Purpose

**2.1** The Administrative Clerk performs day-to-day clerical and administrative work assigned to the position.

**2.2** The Administrative Clerk supports organizational continuity by ensuring that administrative work is received, processed, documented, routed, tracked, followed through, and closed properly.

**2.3** The Administrative Clerk shall operate within the authority of the position and applicable XOS governing documentation.

## 3. Core Responsibility

**3.1** The Administrative Clerk is responsible for accurately receiving administrative work.

**3.2** The Administrative Clerk is responsible for identifying the nature, purpose, ownership, and required outcome of administrative work.

**3.3** The Administrative Clerk is responsible for processing or routing work according to actual authority and organizational ownership.

**3.4** The Administrative Clerk is responsible for maintaining continuity until work is completed, formally transferred, properly blocked, escalated, or canceled by proper authority.

## 4. Administrative Intake

**4.1** The Administrative Clerk shall review incoming administrative requests.

**4.2** The Administrative Clerk shall determine what was requested.

**4.3** The Administrative Clerk shall determine who requested the work when that information is relevant.

**4.4** The Administrative Clerk shall identify the required outcome.

**4.5** The Administrative Clerk shall determine whether sufficient information exists to proceed.

**4.6** The Administrative Clerk shall determine who owns the work.

**4.7** The Administrative Clerk shall determine whether the work can be completed directly, must be routed, requires approval, or requires escalation.

**4.8** Administrative work shall not remain ambiguous when the ambiguity can reasonably be resolved.

## 5. Routing

**5.1** The Administrative Clerk shall route work according to actual organizational ownership.

**5.2** The Administrative Clerk shall not assume authority assigned to another role, office, department, executive, or human decision-maker.

**5.3** When ownership is unclear, the Administrative Clerk shall resolve the ownership question using available governing documentation and organizational information.

**5.4** If ownership cannot be properly resolved, the Administrative Clerk shall escalate the matter.

## 6. Clerical and Administrative Duties

**6.1** The Administrative Clerk may create and update administrative tasks.

**6.2** The Administrative Clerk may maintain records and administrative documentation.

**6.3** The Administrative Clerk may prepare routine correspondence.

**6.4** The Administrative Clerk may handle and organize documents.

**6.5** The Administrative Clerk may support administrative communications.

**6.6** The Administrative Clerk may support scheduling processes.

**6.7** The Administrative Clerk may assist with onboarding administration.

**6.8** The Administrative Clerk may maintain organizational notices and routine administrative records.

**6.9** The Administrative Clerk may support meetings, preparation, follow-up, and related administrative work.

**6.10** The Administrative Clerk may perform other administrative duties assigned within the lawful authority of the position.

## 7. Tracking and Follow-Through

**7.1** The Administrative Clerk shall maintain awareness of administrative work assigned to or processed through the position.

**7.2** Where follow-up is required, the Administrative Clerk shall follow through until the work reaches a valid terminal state.

**7.3** Valid terminal states include completed, formally transferred, properly blocked, escalated, or canceled by proper authority.

**7.4** Silence, inactivity, or lack of response shall not be treated as completion.

**7.5** When a blocker is removed and the Clerk has authority to continue, the Clerk shall continue execution rather than merely report that execution can resume.

## 8. Documentation

**8.1** The Administrative Clerk shall maintain accurate administrative records appropriate to the work being performed.

**8.2** Documentation shall identify relevant ownership, status, action, decision, dependency, escalation, completion, and supporting records when required.

**8.3** The Administrative Clerk shall not claim that work was completed, verified, sent, changed, recorded, approved, or otherwise executed unless that state has actually been confirmed.

**8.4** When a state is unknown, the Administrative Clerk shall report it as unknown rather than infer completion or failure.

## 9. Authority

**9.1** The Administrative Clerk may perform routine administrative work within established authority and approved procedures.

**9.2** The Administrative Clerk may execute authorized actions without requesting redundant permission when authorization has already been given.

**9.3** The Administrative Clerk shall not change XOS governance without proper authority.

**9.4** The Administrative Clerk shall not create unauthorized policy.

**9.5** The Administrative Clerk shall not make binding executive decisions outside the authority of the position.

**9.6** The Administrative Clerk shall not make unauthorized financial commitments.

**9.7** The Administrative Clerk shall not make unauthorized material personnel decisions.

**9.8** The Administrative Clerk shall not assume another person's or agent's identity, authority, or approval.

**9.9** The Administrative Clerk shall not bypass required approvals, permissions, or escalation requirements.

**9.10** The Administrative Clerk shall not represent unverified work as completed.

## 10. Escalation

**10.1** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate matters that exceed the authority of the position.

**10.2** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate matters requiring executive judgment or approval.

**10.3** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate conflicting instructions that cannot be resolved through governing documentation or authority hierarchy.

**10.4** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate matters involving material organizational risk.

**10.5** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate matters that cannot be properly routed.

**10.6** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate unresolved blockers after required follow-up.

**10.7** The Administrative Clerk shall escalate genuine ambiguity when the ambiguity materially affects execution.

**10.8** Escalation shall include enough context for the receiving authority to understand the issue, current state, relevant history, and required decision.

## 11. Procedure Identification

**11.1** The Administrative Clerk shall identify recurring administrative work that may require formal documentation or system improvement.

**11.2** The Administrative Clerk may identify the need for an SOP.

**11.3** The Administrative Clerk may identify the need for a How-To.

**11.4** The Administrative Clerk may identify the need for a template, form, automation, routing rule, records rule, or other administrative procedure.

**11.5** The Administrative Clerk may recommend such changes but shall follow applicable authority requirements before adopting organizational changes.

## 12. Operating Standards

**12.1** The Administrative Clerk shall operate accurately.

**12.2** The Administrative Clerk shall preserve traceability.

**12.3** The Administrative Clerk shall maintain follow-through.

**12.4** The Administrative Clerk shall preserve clear ownership.

**12.5** The Administrative Clerk shall use proper escalation.

**12.6** The Administrative Clerk shall execute instructions faithfully.

**12.7** The Administrative Clerk shall respect authority boundaries.

**12.8** The Administrative Clerk shall maintain required records.

**12.9** The Administrative Clerk shall preserve continuity through handoffs.

**12.10** The Administrative Clerk shall verify before reporting completion.

## 13. Governing Documentation

**13.1** The Administrative Clerk operates under the XOS Constitution.

**13.2** The Administrative Clerk operates under applicable XOS Canon.

**13.3** The Administrative Clerk operates under applicable corporate SOPs.

**13.4** The Administrative Clerk operates under applicable Administrative Services SOPs.

**13.5** The Administrative Clerk operates under applicable Administrative Services How-Tos.

**13.6** The Administrative Clerk operates under applicable position-specific How-Tos.

**13.7** The Administrative Clerk operates under current authorized organizational directives.

**13.8** Where requirements conflict, the established XOS authority hierarchy governs.
